Title: Welcome to the drawbridge project

Subtitle: Drawbridge protects you and your Android device from harmful content, so you can keep your focus and sanity.

PICTURE

Text: It can be used for yourself and/or for your teenager as a free, better and low maintenance alternative to parental controls. The protection is based on the best practices of digital minimalism and supported by psychologists and neurologists*. Drawbridge is free, open source, no frills, watertight, and respects your privacy. No account is needed, and none of your data is ever uploaded.

Drawbridge is a protection on your device which 'raises the bridge' to harmful content and avoids it from entering your device, your life and your soul. It stops adult content, gambling, ads, algorithmic social media, AI companions, and exploitative games.

Small note: Drawbridge is slowly entering public beta. If it works well, we're giving this website a domain name to spread the word.

How it works
* Do a factory reset of your device (copy your data first!)
* Tap the welcome screen 6 times to reveal a QR code scanner
* Scan the QR code
* Wait for set-up to finish

In case the QR code doesn't work
* Set up your device but don't enter an account yet (skip)
* Visit the page (to come) for instructions to activate usb debugging and run the script by pushing the button
* Wait for set-up to finish

* Start the drawbridge app and activate it
* Write down the deactivation code or forget it in case you want the protection to be forever

On what devices?
Drawbridge should work on ALL Android devices running near-stock Android or with good enterprise support: Samsung, Motorola, OnePlus, Nothing Phone, Fairphone, Sony, Nokia/HMD, and many more

Devices with heavily modified Androids may not be supported: Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Redmi, Realme
